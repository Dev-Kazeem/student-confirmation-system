About the Portal

This portal is the enhanced Online Student Confirmation and Document Verification System for Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto.

Purpose

The system allows newly admitted students to complete their confirmation activities online, including submission of personal and academic information, uploading of required documents, and downloading of a verifiable digital confirmation slip.

Key Features
  • Secure student registration and login.
  • Online admission checking.
  • Personal and academic profile submission.
  • Document upload with validation.
  • Real-time application status tracking.
  • Review and approval by admission officers.
  • QR-code-verifiable confirmation slips.
  • Automated notifications and audit logging.
Note

The QR code on a confirmation slip confirms that the slip was generated by this system. It does not by itself certify the authenticity of every uploaded document — final verification is performed by authorized university officers.
